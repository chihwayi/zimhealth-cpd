**Proposal for Strategic Partnership: Enhancing Continuous Professional
Development (CPD) for Zimbabwean Nurses**

**To:** The Registrar and Board, Nurses Council of Zimbabwe (NCZ)

**From:** Healthbeat Private Limited

**Subject:** Proposal for Collaboration on the Healthbeat Continuous
Professional Development Platform

**Executive Summary**

Healthbeat Private Limited requests a strategic partnership with the
Nurses Council of Zimbabwe (NCZ) to enhance the delivery, accessibility,
and management of Continuous Professional Development (CPD) for nurses
nationwide.

While existing systems like the MyCPD platform have laid a foundation
for digital learning, access limitations and constrained course
offerings remain significant challenges for practicing nurses.
The **Healthbeat CPD Platform** addresses these gaps through an
expansive, discipline-specific curriculum and dual-channel access via
both a web portal and WhatsApp -- ensuring every nurse in Zimbabwe can
earn mandatory CPD points regardless of location or internet
connectivity constraints.

Healthbeat's platform architecture already supports multiple regulatory
councils (built to serve NCZ alongside other Zimbabwean health
professional councils on the same technical foundation). This proposal
is written for NCZ specifically, and we welcome discussing preferential
terms for NCZ -- whether an exclusivity window, first-integration-partner
status, or a preferential revenue-share tier -- as part of formal
negotiations.

**Key Strengths & Strategic Value to NCZ**

**1. Dynamic, Comprehensive, and Discipline-Specific Curriculum**

Unlike platforms with limited course catalogs, Healthbeat is building a
continuously expanding library tailored to the diverse nursing landscape
in Zimbabwe. Our curriculum roadmap, ready to populate immediately upon
partnership confirmation and NCZ content sign-off, covers:

**Specialty Nursing Tracks:** Targeted modules for Midwifery, Nursing
Education, Nursing Administration, Operating Theatre Nursing, Mental
Health Nursing, and Pediatric Nursing.

**General Nursing Refresher Modules:** Core competency updates for
general practitioners.

**Cross-Cutting & Emerging Skills:** Modern healthcare courses including
Nursing Research, Data Analytics in Healthcare, Health Informatics, Data
Protection/Privacy, and Nursing Entrepreneurship.

**Continuous Updates:** Content will be regularly updated to reflect
modern clinical guidelines and health priorities, with NCZ reviewing and
signing off on each addition before it reaches practitioners.

**2. Unrivaled Accessibility: Web Portal & WhatsApp Integration**

Limited internet access and high data costs frequently hinder rural and
shift-working nurses from completing CPD units.

**WhatsApp Learning Gateway:** Nurses can complete micro-courses,
quizzes, and reading materials directly within WhatsApp -- drastically
reducing data consumption and removing tech-barrier entry. This channel
is fully built and ready today: activating it for practitioners requires
only provisioning a paid WhatsApp Business number, with no further
development work.

**Web-Based Portal:** A rich web interface for comprehensive studying,
resource downloading, and detailed progress tracking.

**3. Direct NCZ Regulatory Control & Governance**

Healthbeat is built to support -- not replace -- the Council's regulatory
oversight. NCZ administrators are provided with dedicated back-office
access to:

**Content Review & Approval:** Inspect and sign off on all course
materials prior to publication to guarantee alignment with national
nursing standards.

**Point Validation & Audit:** Track nurse compliance and validate CPD
points, with automated compliance reporting for annual re-registration
on our near-term delivery roadmap.

**Commercial & Revenue-Sharing Model**

To ensure seamless transition and financial feasibility for nurses,
Healthbeat proposes matching the current market standard while creating
a sustainable revenue stream for the Council.

**1. User Pricing**

**Annual Subscription:** **USD \$10 per nurse / year** (matching the
current fee on the MyCPD platform to avoid imposing an extra financial
burden on practitioners).

**Included Services:** Unlimited access to all general and specialty
modules, WhatsApp learning access, instant certificate generation, and
automated point sync with NCZ.

**2. Proposed Revenue Split**

Healthbeat proposes a collaborative revenue share from all annual
participant subscriptions:

*The exact percentage split is negotiable and can be customized during
formal negotiations to align with NCZ's administrative requirements.*

**3. Zero Technical Overhead for NCZ**

- Healthbeat covers all platform development, server hosting, payment
  gateway integration, and technical maintenance costs.

- The Council receives guaranteed monthly/quarterly income distributions
  without incurring any operational software expenses.

**Proposed Collaborative Model**

1.  **Content Co-Validation:** NCZ acts as the accrediting body;
    Healthbeat provides the technical infrastructure, content
    development, and continuous platform maintenance.

2.  **System Integration:** Healthbeat's point-sync infrastructure is
    already built and tested; connecting it to NCZ's registration
    database to streamline annual practicing certificate renewals
    requires only an API endpoint and credentials from NCZ's technical
    team, which we would scope together during onboarding.

3.  **Pilot Launch:** A phased rollout beginning with high-demand
    specialty modules to test engagement and gather feedback from
    practicing nurses.

**Next Steps**

We welcome the opportunity to present a live demonstration of the
Healthbeat platform -- including the WhatsApp learning interface -- to the
Council's executive team and discuss the financial framework in detail.
